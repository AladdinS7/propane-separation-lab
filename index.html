01 — THE PROCESS

Separate the phases.
Then recover the propane.

The feed contains propane, n-butane as a heavier hydrocarbon, and water. The flash separates hydrocarbon vapor from liquid; the water is modeled as a separate free-water phase.

The middle condensate outlet feeds a depropanizer-style tower. Its overhead collects propane, while the heavier hydrocarbon leaves in the bottoms.

01 ↑

Top · Propane-rich gas

The more volatile component favors the vapor phase. This gas is a recovered propane stream, but it is not necessarily pure propane.

02 →

Middle · Condensate to tower

A hydrocarbon liquid carries propane and n-butane to the recovery tower. Its flow can fall to zero when the hydrocarbon feed is all vapor.

03 ↓

Bottom · Free water

All feed water is routed here in the ideal decanter assumption. The model does not certify water purity or calculate dissolved propane.

02 — THE FLASH

Let equilibrium set the split.

Antoine vapor-pressure K-values approximate the propane/n-butane vapor–liquid partition. The Rachford–Rice equation then finds the hydrocarbon vapor fraction β between zero and one.

Kᵢ = Pˢᵃᵗᵢ(T) / P
log₁₀(Pˢᵃᵗᵢ/bar) = Aᵢ − Bᵢ/(T + Cᵢ)
Σ zᵢ (Kᵢ − 1) / [1 + β(Kᵢ − 1)] = 0

T in kelvin and P in bar; NIST Antoine coefficients are used within their fitted ranges. z is the normalized hydrocarbon composition. Single-phase endpoints are checked before solving.

03 — THE TOWER

A recovery scenario you control.

The tower is a component splitter: you specify how much of each hydrocarbon reaches the overhead. This makes the material balance transparent without claiming a rigorous distillation solution.

Product C₃ = condensate C₃ × assumed C₃ recovery
Product C₄ = condensate C₄ × assumed C₄ carryover

Tray symbols explain the equipment. Reflux, stages, reboiler duty, and tower pressure are not solved. A rigorous design needs a validated property package and column calculation.

A little vocabulary. A clearer picture.

Recovery
The fraction of feed propane collected in the flash gas and tower overhead. High recovery does not automatically mean high purity.
Purity
The propane mole fraction within a particular outlet. Always check which stream a purity value refers to.
Condensate
The liquid hydrocarbon phase in the separator, distinct from the water phase. It may contain both propane and n-butane.
Vapor fraction, β
The fraction of the hydrocarbon feed leaving the flash as vapor. Water is excluded from this calculation.
Three-phase separator
A vessel with gas, hydrocarbon-liquid, and water outlets. Three outlets do not guarantee three flowing phases for every input.
Material balance
What enters must leave. This app checks each component across the separator, tower, and full process.

EDITABLE EQUILIBRIUM STAGES · A SEPARATE STUDY

How much separation can stages provide?

An ideal equilibrium stage lets its leaving vapor and liquid reach equilibrium. Use the stage-count input to explore the Fenske total-reflux limit. This does not replace the specified tower splits in the stream ledger.

How this stage estimate works

We hold the reference bottoms composition from your split case and use α = K₍C₃₎/K₍C₄₎ at the flash temperature as a constant volatility approximation. Real column temperatures and α vary. Count an equilibrium reboiler as one stage; exclude a total condenser.

Nₘᵢₙ = ln[(xᴅ/(1−xᴅ)) / (xʙ/(1−xʙ))] / ln(α)
At N stages: overhead odds = bottoms odds × αᴺ

Total reflux is a limiting condition with no feed or product withdrawal. This curve shows ideal limiting compositions, not flowing products or an operating column prediction. A real column at finite reflux generally needs more stages. The fractional minimum is rounded up for a chosen stage count.

02 — THE FLASH

Let equilibrium set the split.

Antoine vapor-pressure K-values approximate the propane/n-butane vapor–liquid partition. The Rachford–Rice equation then finds the hydrocarbon vapor fraction β between zero and one.

Kᵢ = Pˢᵃᵗᵢ(T) / P
log₁₀(Pˢᵃᵗᵢ/bar) = Aᵢ − Bᵢ/(T + Cᵢ)
Σ zᵢ (Kᵢ − 1) / [1 + β(Kᵢ − 1)] = 0

T in kelvin and P in bar; NIST Antoine coefficients are used within their fitted ranges. z is the normalized hydrocarbon composition. Single-phase endpoints are checked before solving.

03 — THE TOWER

A recovery scenario you control.

The tower is a component splitter: you specify how much of each hydrocarbon reaches the overhead. This makes the material balance transparent without claiming a rigorous distillation solution.

Product C₃ = condensate C₃ × assumed C₃ recovery
Product C₄ = condensate C₄ × assumed C₄ carryover

Tray symbols explain the equipment. Reflux, stages, reboiler duty, and tower pressure are not solved. A rigorous design needs a validated property package and column calculation.

What does “likely to fail” mean?

HYPOTHETICAL TEACHING DATA
These probabilities are conditional on your assumptions. Default lifetimes are invented examples, not field measurements or manufacturer ratings. This is the probability of a listed component’s first functional failure. It is not a leak, fire, explosion, or injury probability, and it is not inferred from the flash temperature or pressure.

The period you want to study. Try 1,000 h, then 8,000 h.

MTTF = mean time to first failure, in hours. Every checked component is included in “any first failure.”

THE EQUATION

Longer exposure means more opportunity.

λᵢ = 1 / MTTFᵢ
Pᵢ(t) = 1 − exp(−λᵢt)
P(any listed failure) = 1 − exp(−t Σλᵢ)

A 10% probability means that, among many identical systems under these assumptions, about 10 in 100 would experience at least one listed first failure by that time. It does not predict the exact time an individual component will fail.

Do not add the percentages: events can overlap. The combined expression accounts for overlap under independence.

WHEN THE MODEL FITS

Know what is missing.

The exponential model assumes a constant failure rate. Component events are independent; the units begin functioning at time zero. The calculation considers first failures and excludes repairs. “Any listed failure” is a system failure only if each listed part is essential and the first failure stops the system.

A real study needs defined failure modes, relevant service history or manufacturer data, uncertainty bounds, and treatment of common causes, aging, maintenance, and redundancy. Only the selected parts are included.

YOUR CURRENT EDITS

Why can’t this case run?

Rejected values versus useful warnings

Rejected: incomplete numbers, negative flow, percentages outside 0–100, a feed total other than 100%, conditions outside the model range, or a noninteger stage count. Correct these before calculating.

Allowed with explanation: zero feed, an idle tower, no water, poor propane enrichment, or a high-pressure ideal-model warning. Use Review & run to read the feedback, then choose Continue with my values if you want to study that case.

Unknown to this app: your vessel’s design pressure, construction materials, relief protection, real failure rates, and a column’s validated operating limits. The app does not turn its input bounds into an equipment approval.
